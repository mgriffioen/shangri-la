Connecting Google Photos

  • Album link — use the album’s share link (Share → Create link). Anyone in the crew with the link can open the full album.
  • Cover photo — Google doesn’t let sites embed whole albums anymore, so pick one great photo per year: open it, right-click, “Copy image address”, paste it as the cover URL.
